How we measure

Numbers need context to mean something.

Revenue vs. profit

Sales revenue is not net profit. Product cost, shipping, ad spend, fees and refunds affect profitability.

Attribution

Platform-reported results can differ due to attribution windows, tracking setup and cross-channel activity.

Evidence
